# Visual audit of generated story art

Reviewed the six full-size covers, invitation artwork, 43 exported portraits and
22 scene/detail assets against their story descriptions. Covers, invitation and
portraits were retained: no clear defects requiring replacement were found in
this pass. Inspection is a visual/editorial review, not a guarantee that every
illustrative detail is physically exact.

Six scene assets replaced:

- bakery: corrected the gingerbread brand to КОВАЛЁВЪ, including the final hard sign;
- ashes: restored the readable surviving foil text …ГОКСИН 0,25 мг;
- broken-bridge: filled the blank sign with РЕМОНТ / ОБЪЕЗД / ПО НОВОМУ МОСТУ;
- kostya: restored eyebrow plaster and replaced the impossible back-facing
  camera-holder reflection with a cropped wrist/hand at the right edge;
- shmel: replaced the uninformative rear view with a cropped eye-level scene
  fragment showing the gaze toward the doorway;
- gruzdev-threat: corrected the retracted prop to a short visible blade without
  contact or injury.

The first regenerated Kostya candidate still contained the impossible reflected
head and was rejected after review/user feedback. It is not used in the game.
Original generations and rejected candidates remain outside the public assets.
All replacements were made with image generation/editing; exports only resize
and encode approved files. The eye-level fragment retains its natural 2:1 aspect
ratio (720x360), recorded in the visual catalog; other replacements are 720x480.
Original captions, story branches, portraits and progress remain unchanged.

Verified encoded files, dimensions, published caption/asset coverage and browser
decoding at mobile widths. Story clues remain available in the original readable
captions. Ordinary hostkey.sh update deploys this asset correction.
